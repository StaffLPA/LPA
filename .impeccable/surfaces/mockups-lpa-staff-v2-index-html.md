# Surface brief: Meet the Staff v2 (mockup)

Scope: mockups/lpa-staff-v2/index.html. Replaces the dark lineup-card mockup at the user's request ("some font are unable to see because it is black and black is the background ... convert to parents and athletes ... modern"). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: parents of prospective players and the athletes themselves. Action: Book a tour (legendaryprepacademy.com/appointments).
Proof: real staff records from the live site, studio portraits, five playing-days photos. Nothing invented.
Constraints: LPA brand (black, rust #AB562B, House of Cards display, LPA shield); every text colour set on its own surface; static HTML on the taste-skill kit.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The staff page sells the tour through people: former pros for the athlete, the adults who drive, feed and look after him for the parent. It refuses an even grid of identical portrait cards on black.
OWN-WORLD: Light neutral ground (#f4f4f1) with black ink, LPA rust for actions and roles, one full-bleed black band for the playing careers. House of Cards uppercase for the h1 and h2, IBM Plex Sans for reading. Studio portraits with rounded corners; flat colour, no gradients.
STORY: The first screen names who coaches (former pros, Scottsdale) and the tour; next the coaches, their careers, the people who look after players day to day, the front office, then the tour.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Split hero, words left (h1, a lead with the hard facts, Book a tour), the head coach's portrait right with one float card of his record.
FORM: seed: none (taste-skill deal). The composition was dealt by the design-taste-frontend dealer, recorded in the comment at the top of index.html: hero split, nav centre, close type, footer columns, rhythm dark-band, buttons tonal, moment picture-develops. The user set the brief (light, readable, converts parents and athletes, keep the brand), not the layout.
SIGNATURE: In the black careers band, the playing-days cards sit beside a large photo of Taylor Hubbell pitching, which develops like a print as it comes on screen.
