# Surface brief: Meet the Staff (mockup)

Scope: mockups/lpa-staff/index.html, a redesign of legendaryprepacademy.com/staff. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: parents of prospective players deciding whether to trust LPA. Action: Book a tour (legendaryprepacademy.com/appointments).
Proof: the 16 real staff records from the live site, credentials verbatim, studio portraits and five playing-days photos. Nothing invented.
Constraints: keep the brand (black ground, rust #AB562B, seafoam #40BF9D, House of Cards, LPA shield and arch wordmark). Static HTML/CSS.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The staff page is LPA's dugout lineup card: the whole staff readable at a glance as one roster, each name opening a full career. It refuses the category's grid of identical portrait cards with three bullets and a "+2 more".
OWN-WORLD: Black card stock with printed rust rule lines and column heads, row numbers in the left gutter, printed section bands (Starters, Front office, Clubhouse, Partners) as on a real lineup card. House of Cards for every printed label and name; seafoam only for the marker that circles the selected row and for focus. Portraits sit in a frame clipped to the card's edge, like a photo paper-clipped to the card.
STORY: Within one viewport a parent sees how many real professionals run the program, then picks a name and reads the career in full.
FORM: seed:725bae0a680d7b9b. Assigned grounded direction 5 (lineup card), chosen by the user; degraded roll, no challengers.
SIGNATURE: Selecting a row circles it in seafoam marker and slides that person's portrait and full career into the frame beside the card; the playing-days photo sits in the row's own Then column.
CLOSE: The card's printed footer carries the tour: "Book a tour" with the address and phone.
