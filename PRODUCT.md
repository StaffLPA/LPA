## Stack

## Users

Primary: parents of prospective players, deciding whether to trust Legendary Prep Academy with their son's baseball development and his schooling. They read carefully, compare programs, and look for people they can trust.

Secondary: the players themselves, and college coaches and scouts judging the program's credibility.

## Product Purpose

Legendary Prep Academy (LPA) is a baseball prep academy in Scottsdale, Arizona. Players train every weekday morning (cages, glove work, lifting, throwing programs, speed) and complete accredited online school (EdOptions) on site in the afternoon, with lunch, mandatory recovery and pick-up built into one schedule. Success: families book a campus tour.

## Positioning

The site calls LPA "Arizona's first professional baseball prep academy." Its staff is the proof: coaches with real professional careers (a first-round MLB pick and former MLB bullpen coach, a current Houston Astros area scout, minor-league All-Stars), Division 1 college players, certified strength coaches, plus an administration and executive team with military, fire-service and business backgrounds.

## Operating Context

Visitors reach the staff page from the main nav (Home, The LPA Experience, Meet the Staff, Schedule, Partnerships, Fan Gear, Apply, Contact Us, Book Tour). The current page's call to action is "Request LPA Program Guide"; the nav's primary button is "Book Tour" (/appointments).

## Brand Commitments

The user asked to keep the site's branding. Binding assets: the LPA shield (black shield, rust border, seafoam cactus), the rust "LPA" arch wordmark, the outlaw mascot, the House of Cards typeface (self-hosted on the site), black ground, rust primary (hsl 20 60% 42%, about #AB562B) and seafoam accent (hsl 164 50% 50%, about #40BF9D). Staff portraits are a consistent studio series: black LPA polos against warm orange bokeh.

## Product Principles

1. Credentials are the argument: show real careers specifically, never in vague praise.
2. Write for a parent's trust: plain, specific, and verifiable.
3. Every page ends at the tour.
4. The brand already exists. Extend it; don't replace it.
