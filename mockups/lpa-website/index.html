The LPA school day, hour by hour

  1. Morning training in the cages and weight room

    Groups rotate between hitting, defence and a throwing program, with pitchers under Steve Karsay and strength work with Josh Garcia.

  2. Afternoon classroom with EdOptions

    Players work through accredited online high school in the classroom, with Martin Jacquez tracking grades and eligibility.

  3. Team lunch, recovery and pick-up

    A cooked lunch from The Creative Plate, mandatory recovery and Pilates with Vinna's therapists, and pick-up at 2:30 or 3:00.

Admissions questions from parents

Does my son still earn a high school diploma?

Yes. Classes run through EdOptions, an accredited online high school, during the school day at LPA.

Who can apply?

Players from eighth grade to senior year. Middle school players join Coach Hubbell's group.

Where does training happen?

At the academy in Scottsdale, with Tuesday speed sessions at Bell 94.

How do parents stay in touch with coaches?

Through the LPA Hub app: messages, the weekly schedule, forms and announcements in one place.

What does a year cost?

Tuition depends on the team and the lunch plan. The front office sends the fee sheet after a first call.
